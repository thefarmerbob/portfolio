About Chihiro

In a world full of stoners and alcoholics, my drug of choice is music. I listen to the same song over and over for an entire week, then rarely touch it again. When I play a song from an "era" of mine, it brings me the heaviest nostalgia — it's the way I remember places, people, food, and experiences.

Chihiro is a nostalgia-driven music recommendation system. It predicts songs that maximize my nostalgia based on semantic similarity between my current mood and past journal entries associated with my listening patterns.

309 HANDWRITTEN JOURNAL PAGES
32,278 SPOTIFY STREAMS
324 UNIQUE SONGS
MODELS: TF-IDF · SBERT · VAE · LIME

Built with fine-tuned Sentence-BERT, a Variational Autoencoder, and LIME interpretability. The models learned that writing about dumplings and claw machines meant the Ylang Ylang era, and writing about mom and bus rides meant Hell N Back season.

* This visual demo uses the Claude API for recommendations, not the trained notebook models.
